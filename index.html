01 · Profile

Gas, fluid and rock data, turned into exploration models

I am a geochemist specialising in subsurface hydrogen (H₂), helium (He) and carbon dioxide (CO₂) systems, with experience across academic research at Caltech and Oxford and applied resource assessment at the Bureau of Indian Affairs.

I combine field mapping, laboratory analysis (noble gas and isotope-ratio mass spectrometry, gas chromatography) and quantitative modelling in MATLAB, Python, Petra and ArcGIS to turn raw gas, fluid and rock data into exploration models and resource assessments.

First-author publications in Geochimica et Cosmochimica Acta and Chemical Geology; experienced in securing funding, leading field teams and training colleagues.
